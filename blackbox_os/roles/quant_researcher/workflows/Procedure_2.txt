The Senior Quant Researcher Catalog (Architecture, Governance, & Tradeoffs)
The Senior persona designs the quantitative research methodology, establishes rigorous statistical standards, prevents empirical biases (p-hacking, lookahead, survivorship), and owns the business risk of technical trade-offs before code handoff.

Stage 1 — Research Framing & Literature
hypothesis_statement_formalizer: Translates vague market ideas into mathematically testable null and alternative hypotheses.
prior_literature_search: Evaluates academic and industry research to establish baseline theoretical foundations.
literature_replication_scaffold: Builds standardized reproduction pipelines for peer-reviewed quantitative strategies.
economic_rationale_check: Ensures signals possess an underlying structural or behavioral market economic mechanism.
idea_prioritization_scoring: Ranks research proposals based on expected Sharpe, capacity, and firm strategic fit.
benchmark_selection_justification: Mandates risk-matched, investable benchmarks to prevent artificial alpha claims.

Stage 2 — Data Ingestion & Point-in-Time Integrity
tick_data_ingestion: Establishes tick processing policy and memory-efficient streaming architectures.
missing_data_imputation: Sets institutional standards for handling missing market data without leaking future state.
outlier_detection_price: Distinguishes genuine market flash-events from bad exchange tick data feeds.
duplicate_record_removal: Deduplicates order book and trade data while preserving high-frequency event sequences.
timestamp_alignment: Owns timezone normalization and microsecond alignment across multi-venue datasets.
cross_asset_data_normalization: Standardizes heterogeneous asset data into unified numerical formats.
instrument_mapping: Owns symbology mapping across vendor tickers, CUSIPs, ISINs, and exchange IDs.
exchange_calendar_handling: Manages complex global trading calendars, holiday schedules, and half-days.
corporate_action_adjustment: Dictates backward/forward adjustment policies for splits, dividends, and spin-offs.
seasonality_adjustment: Filters out predictable intra-day/intra-week volume and volatility cyclicality.
point_in_time_snapshotting: Enforces point-in-time database queries to strictly eliminate lookahead leakage.
data_freshness_monitor: Establishes latency SLA thresholds for research data pipeline feeds.
reference_data_diffing: Audits changes in vendor master tables over time for silent restatements.
data_source_reconciliation: Reconciles discrepancies across competing market data providers.
cross_dataset_join_audit: Catches silent data loss and row inflation during complex relational joins.
dataset_versioning: Locks historical dataset hashes for immutable backtest reproduction.
data_lineage_documentation: Tracks feature origin graphs from raw exchange feeds to final alpha vectors.
data_privacy_compliance_check: Ensures alternative data sources comply with regulatory and privacy standards.
data_vendor_cost_benefit: Evaluates financial ROI of purchasing new commercial alternative datasets.
currency_normalization: Converts multi-currency global asset series to base reporting currency.

Stage 3 — Feature & Signal Engineering
rolling_window_feature_builder: Constructs leak-free rolling statistical features across dynamic lookback windows.
lagged_feature_construction: Ensures proper timestamp shifting for predictive indicators.
feature_scaling_pointintime: Fits scaling parameters exclusively within past rolling windows.
feature_orthogonalization: Decorrelates candidate alpha factors against existing risk factors using Gram-Schmidt/PCA.
cross_sectional_ranking: Applies cross-sectional z-scoring and rank transformations across asset universes.
alt_data_relevance_screen: Evaluates signal-to-noise ratios of unstructured alternative data streams.
event_study_construction: Builds parametric abnormal return windows around discrete market catalysts.

Stage 4 — Statistical Testing & Hypothesis Audit
stationarity_test: Audits time series for unit roots and dictates differencing/cointegration procedures.
autocorrelation_diagnostic: Measures residual temporal dependence to prevent inflated t-statistics.
multicollinearity_check: Identifies variance inflation factors across correlated predictive signals.
p_hacking_self_audit: Tracks total candidate trials to adjust significance thresholds against data mining.
multiple_testing_correction: Applies Benjamini-Hochberg / Bonferroni adjustments to empirical p-values.
multiple_hypothesis_tracker: Logs every tested signal iteration to calculate total hypothesis space search cost.
permutation_test: Scrambles signal timestamps to establish non-parametric baseline noise distributions.
deflated_sharpe_ratio: Computes Sharpe probability adjusted for track record length and trial count.
statistical_vs_economic_significance: Evaluates whether statistical alpha survives realistic transaction costs.
sample_size_sufficiency_check: Conducts power analysis to verify minimum statistical sample requirements.
bootstrap_confidence_interval: Generates robust confidence bands around backtest performance statistics.
lookahead_bias_audit: Rigorously scans backtest logic for subtle future-information leaks.
survivorship_bias_quantification: Measures performance bias introduced by excluding delisted assets.
survivorship_bias_check: Verifies historical universe membership accurately includes dead/merged assets.
survivorship_free_index_construction: Reconstructs historical index constituent lists at point-in-time dates.
sample_period_selection_justification: Ensures training sample start dates are not cherry-picked.

Stage 5 — Backtesting & Cross-Validation Design
backtest_walkforward_split: Designs expanding/rolling walk-forward out-of-sample test splits.
backtest_engine_validation: Audits event-driven backtesting engine execution matching logic.
walk_forward_reoptimization: Tests adaptive hyperparameter retraining schedules across historical regimes.
out_of_sample_validation: Enforces strict holdout validation gates before strategy approval.
cross_validation_time_series: Implements Combinatorial Purged & Embargoed Cross-Validation.
benchmark_index_replication_check: Verifies strategy beta tracking error against target benchmark indices.
benchmark_drawdown_comparison: Evaluates strategy relative drawdown profile versus benchmark crises.

Stage 6 — Signal Combination & Regime Modeling
signal_combination_methodology: Blends heterogeneous signals using robust optimization (Equal Risk, Mean-Var).
factor_exposure_decomposition: Decomposes returns into style factors (Value, Momentum, Quality, Size).
factor_zoo_screening: Filters candidate signals against standard factor libraries to isolate true alpha.
macro_regime_classifier: Trains hidden Markov / clustering models to classify macroeconomic regimes.
regime_labeling: Annotates historical periods into volatility and liquidity structural states.
regime_conditional_performance: Evaluates strategy Sharpe and returns split by market regime.
regime_break_stress_test: Simulates strategy performance during catastrophic historical regime shifts.
rolling_correlation_breakdown: Monitors temporal decay in pairwise factor correlations.
strategy_correlation_matrix: Computes pairwise correlation across all active firm strategies.
correlation_to_existing_book: Mandates low correlation (<0.3) against existing trading books before allocation.
feature_importance_stability: Checks if model feature weights remain stable across rolling time windows.
subsample_stability_check: Tests parameter consistency across different random data subsamples.
parameter_sensitivity_scan: Plots parameter response surfaces to catch brittle optimization cliffs.

Stage 7 — Capacity, Decay & Microstructure Simulation
transaction_cost_modeling: Models non-linear market impact (square-root law) and venue fee schedules.
slippage_simulation: Simulates order book sweep costs and queue fill delays under volume stress.
bid_ask_sanity_check: Verifies spread capture assumptions reflect realistic order types (Maker vs Taker).
turnover_accounting: Tracks annualized strategy turnover and computes marginal trading drag.
capacity_estimation: Estimates maximum AUM capacity before alpha decays below hurdle rate.
capacity_decay_monitor: Tracks alpha degradation as strategy capital scales.
signal_decay_analysis: Measures half-life of signal predictive power post-bar generation.
model_degradation_monitor: Establishes statistical process control bounds for live vs backtest tracking error.
drawdown_analysis: Decomposes drawdown depth, duration, and recovery time statistics.
drawdown_scenario_replay: Replays extreme historical liquidity crises (2008, 2020, 2022) on strategy vectors.
tail_risk_estimation: Calculates Expected Shortfall (CVaR) and extreme value tail probabilities.
leverage_margin_impact_check: Models margin maintenance calls under stressed volatility spikes.
cost_of_capital_hurdle_check: Verifies net returns exceed institutional cost of capital and risk-free rates.

Stage 8 — Handoff, Reproducibility & Governance
research_writeup_template: Generates standardized institutional research reports for investment committee.
peer_review_checklist: Executes mandatory cross-team quantitative review prior to production code conversion.
research_to_production_handoff: Translates python research artifacts into compiled production specifications.
paper_trading_setup: Configures live paper-trading environment to monitor out-of-sample forward drift.
negative_result_logging: Documents failed research hypotheses to prevent duplicate team effort.
experiment_tracking_log: Logs all model parameters, commit hashes, and metrics in MLflow/Weights&Biases.
signal_novelty_scoring: Quantifies novelty of proposed signal relative to existing IP library.
assumption_documentation: Explicitly records structural assumptions, failure modes, and risk caveats.
reproducibility_packaging: Containers strategy code, data dependencies, and random seeds into Docker images.
version_controlled_research_repo: Enforces Git flow standards and versioning on research codebase.
environment_reproducibility_lock: Locks pip/conda dependency trees to freeze execution environment.
unit_consistency_check: Audits mathematical units (bps vs decimal, daily vs annualized) across calculations.
compute_cost_tracking: Tracks cloud CPU/GPU spend per research experiment.
data_pipeline_scheduling: Sets cron execution dependencies for automated research signal updates.
postmortem_analysis: Conducts postmortem audits when live paper performance deviates from backtest.
