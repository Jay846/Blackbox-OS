The Senior Agent Catalog (Architecture, Governance, & Tradeoffs)
The Senior persona designs the approach and sets the institutional standards. It handles edge cases, reviews mid/junior output, decides when a skill should NOT be used, and owns the business risk of technical trade-offs.
Stage 1 — Problem Framing
problem_statement_formalizer: Decides whether the problem is even ML-solvable vs. needing a simpler rules-based fix; owns final scope sign-off.
target_variable_definition: Arbitrates between competing definitions with different business implications; catches target definitions that leak future information.
success_metric_selection: Owns the metric-to-business-outcome translation, including explaining complex trade-offs to non-technical executives.
baseline_establishment: Uses baseline results to ruthlessly kill projects early when the performance ceiling isn't worth pursuing.
stakeholder_requirement_capture: Anticipates production and operational constraints stakeholders don't know to ask for yet (regulatory, ethical, hardware limits).
feasibility_scoping: Makes the final go/no-go engineering call and owns the operational consequences of being wrong.
Stage 2 — Data Ingestion & Integrity
mean_imputation: Sets organizational data engineering policy for when mean imputation is acceptable at all.
median_imputation: Reviews whether imputation should happen at all vs. treating missingness as its own explicitly modeled signal.
knn_imputation: Decides when KNN's heavy inference-time computational cost is justified vs. simpler, faster methods.
mice_imputation: Decides when MICE's training complexity is warranted vs. overkill; reviews pipeline engineering feasibility at scale.
forward_fill_imputation: Sets strict policy for how stale a forward-filled value can get before it must be treated as completely missing again.
missing_indicator_flagging: Diagnoses systematic MNAR (Missing Not At Random) patterns that make missingness flags architecturally essential.
train_test_leakage_scan: Designs the institutional leakage-scanning architectural process itself and trains the team to recognize subtle cases.
target_leakage_detector: Catches highly disguised leakage hidden through indirect mathematical derivation, several graph steps removed from the target.
duplicate_row_detector: Dictates organizational policy for near-duplicates that represent legitimately separate historical events rather than infrastructure bugs.
temporal_leakage_check: Owns the point-in-time data architecture (e.g., feature store time-travel queries) that makes this checkable at scale.
group_leakage_check: Catches complex multi-level grouping issues (household vs. individual vs. device) that standard single keys completely miss.
Stage 3 — Exploratory Analysis
distribution_visualizer: Recognizes complex distribution shapes indicating deeper upstream data-generation failures, not just modeling choices.
correlation_heatmap_builder: Distinguishes genuine multi-variable multicollinearity from spurious correlation driven entirely by an unmeasured confounder.
outlier_visual_inspection: Sets organizational data governance policy for how outliers are handled by default per business line and data type.
class_balance_summary: Weighs algorithmic imbalance-handling against the business cost of simply paying to collect more minority-class data.
missingness_pattern_plotter: Diagnoses systemic data-collection infrastructure failures revealed by non-random missingness patterns.
pairplot_generator: Uses observed visual multi-feature layouts to hypothesize explicit mathematical interaction terms the model should be fed.
summary_statistics_reporter: Uses this report as the primary checkpoint in a broader data-quality data sign-off before engineering resources are unblocked.
Stage 4 — Preprocessing & Features
standard_scaler_apply: Catches subtle scale-leakage where transformation parameters were fit on the full dataset instead of strictly inside train folds.
minmax_scaler_apply: Anticipates and builds architecture for how new incoming production data outside the training range breaks a minmax pipeline.
robust_scaler_apply: Balances robust scaling implementations against actually forcing an engineering fix on the upstream outlier-generating hardware/process.
one_hot_encoding: Decides when one-hot is structurally the wrong architectural choice entirely (very high cardinality, ordinal or tree setups).
target_encoding: Deeply reviews data architecture specifically for leakage—the single most common source of falsely inflated backtests.
ordinal_encoding: Catches features that look ordinal but where the true target relationship scales non-linearly with the assigned integers.
log_transform_apply: Recognizes when a mathematical transform is accidentally masking a severe data-quality issue rather than fixing legitimate physical skew.
recursive_feature_elimination: Judges when RFE's greedy elimination strategy is trapped in a local minimum and missing better multi-feature subsets.
mutual_information_selection: Uses near-zero MI across the board diagnostically—it often means the target variable itself is fundamentally poorly defined.
chi_square_feature_selection: Weighs statistical significance against practical, real-world business significance before dropping variables.
lasso_feature_selection: Understands Lasso's structural instability under highly correlated features and cross-checks selections using alternative paradigms.
variance_threshold_filter: Verifies that low-variance features are not rare-but-critical operational signals (e.g., high-value fraud flags) before dropping them.
forward_stepwise_selection: Cross-validates against alternative selection methods, knowing forward selection's greediness can miss optimal multi-feature interactions.
backward_elimination_selection: Decides which feature selection paradigm fits the project's overall interpretability, regulatory compliance, and compute budgets.
smote_oversampling: Decides whether synthetic oversampling is structurally appropriate at all vs. cost-sensitive learning or data acquisition.
random_undersampling: Chooses undersampling only when majority-class data is genuinely redundant, not just because it is abundant.
class_weight_balancing: Translates complex, real-world business liability and financial costs directly into the optimization loss re-weighting scheme.
adasyn_oversampling: Diagnoses whether boundary-focused oversampling genuinely helps out-of-sample generalization or simply overfits to noisy boundary anomalies.
tomek_links_cleaning: Evaluates whether boundary-cleaning helps live generalization or just makes the cross-validation metrics look falsely optimistic.
balanced_bagging_ensemble: Chooses this architecture over data-level resampling to avoid double-correcting for class imbalances across pipelines.
Stage 5 — Validation Design
kfold_cross_validation: Recognizes when standard k-fold is completely invalid for the data structure (time-series, grouped, spatial) and dictates the correct variant.
stratified_kfold_validation: Extends stratification design patterns to multi-label or multi-output problems where standard libraries fail to automate it.
leave_one_out_validation: Knows LOO's mathematical pathologies (high error-estimate variance) and when to explicitly forbid its use despite intuitive appeal.
nested_cross_validation: Justifies nested CV's heavy computational and timeline cost to business stakeholders wanting faster iteration.
bootstrap_validation: Mathematical distinguishes bootstrap-estimated uncertainty from true out-of-sample uncertainty (which bootstrap can understate).
holdout_validation: Sets strict organizational technical policy for when holdout's simplicity is worth its higher structural variance.
time_series_cross_validation: Sets the organizational infrastructure standard here—this is the single most common source of falsely optimistic backtests.
repeated_kfold_validation: Balances diminishing statistical returns of extra repeats against the compute budget allocated for the entire project.
Stage 6 — Model Building
random_forest_builder: Decides when a random forest is structurally the wrong tool entirely (very high-dimensional sparse data, need for extrapolation).
gradient_boosting_builder: Chooses between competing boosting frameworks (XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost) based on raw categorical cardinality and size.
stacking_ensemble_builder: Judges whether an ensemble's added technical debt, latency, and leakage risk are worth a marginal performance gain.
bagging_ensemble_builder: Recognizes when bagging won't help model performance because the core error is bias-dominated rather than variance-dominated.
voting_classifier_builder: Weighs the structural interpretability and maintenance cost of multi-model inference against the performance gain for stakeholders.
blending_ensemble_builder: Chooses blending over stacking specifically when production speed and training wall-clock time outweigh marginal robustness.
l1_lasso_regularization: Understands Lasso's structural instability under highly correlated features and dictates when Elastic Net must be used instead.
l2_ridge_regularization: Chooses Ridge over Lasso when sparsity isn't required but high stability under heavy multicollinearity is mandatory.
elastic_net_regularization: Decides when the added operational hyperparameter tuning complexity is genuinely worth it over a single-penalty approach.
dropout_regularization: Decides where dropout helps vs. hurts architecturally across network graphs (e.g., interaction near output layers vs small setups).
early_stopping_regularization: Recognizes when early stopping is masking an underlying learning-rate schedule bug—and when it can kill a model right before it generalizes.
weight_decay_regularization: Understands the mathematical interaction between weight decay and adaptive optimizers (like AdamW) well enough to use it deliberately, not as a default.
max_norm_constraint: Chooses max-norm over weight decay for network architectures highly prone to exploding gradients (e.g., custom RNNs or GANs).
Stage 7 — Tuning & Evaluation
grid_search_tuner: Recognizes when grid search's exponential combinatorial cost makes it an irresponsible choice for the team's compute budget.
random_search_tuner: Sets the total random sampling budget from a principled diminishing-returns power analysis, never a random round number.
bayesian_optimization_tuner: Judges when the engineering setup overhead of Bayesian optimization loops is worth the return—typically reserved for heavy models.
hyperband_tuner: Decides whether Hyperband's core mathematical assumption actually holds true for this specific class of custom model architectures.
population_based_training: Justifies substantial parallel cluster compute costs against simpler optimization alternatives for the project's bottom-line budget.
optuna_study_runner: Sets the organization's default hyperparameter tuning frameworks, distributed infrastructure backends, and study-design conventions.
precision_recall_calculator: Translates algorithmic precision/recall trade-offs into precise risk, financial liability, or revenue language executives can act on.
f1_score_calculator: Chooses whether F1, F-beta, or a completely custom business-weighted cost metric mathematically matches the true corporate objective function.
roc_auc_calculator: Decides which metric family matches the true underlying class balance, data collection constraints, and downstream application logic.
confusion_matrix_builder: Uses observed macroscopic confusion patterns to completely redesign the target variable definition or upstream data pipeline.
log_loss_calculator: Diagnoses poor log loss scores despite high flat accuracy as a fundamental probability calibration problem, not a discrimination flaw.
matthews_correlation_coefficient: Explains to non-technical stakeholders why MCC, rather than baseline accuracy, should drive the final go/no-go production deployment decision.
calibration_curve_builder: Decides which live use cases strictly require calibrated probabilities (e.g., risk pricing, capital allocation) vs. simple correct ranking.
Stage 8 — Interpretation & Deployment
shap_value_explainer: Uses SHAP aggregation findings to challenge a model's foundational validity when it mathematically relies on features that shouldn't causally matter.
permutation_importance_calculator: Uses heavy divergence between different feature importance calculation methods as a high-priority signal of a deeper multi-feature systemic flaw.
partial_dependence_plotter: Uses identified PDP anomalies (e.g., jagged peaks) to catch underlying pipeline transformation bugs or uncover genuinely novel data regimes.
lime_explainer: Understands LIME's run-to-run random seed instability and knows when that specific behavioral flaw makes it unsuitable for audited regulatory environments.
feature_importance_ranker: Uses macro feature importance rankings to guide what enterprise data acquisition or infrastructure investment the company should make next.
model_drift_monitor: Establishes the operational response protocol and engineer page schedules when a model drift alert fires (e.g., trigger retrain, auto-rollback).
data_drift_monitor: Owns whether data drift requires system retraining or reflects the model correctly adapting to real, structurally informative macroeconomic changes.
ab_test_evaluator: Owns the final ship/no-ship production decision, carefully weighing statistical significance against actual operational practical significance.
champion_challenger_setup: Owns the architectural rollback plan, traffic-routing safety thresholds, and acceptable system risk profiles for the live cutover process itself.
model_version_registry: Owns the registry's strict automated governance gates—specifies what artifacts, metadata, and testing metrics must lock before any model hits production.
latency_monitoring: Owns the macroscopic latency budget tradeoff against model architectural complexity from day one of model selection, not just at deployment time.
